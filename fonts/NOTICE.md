The `.ttf` files are modified: each variable font was instanced to fixed
weights (400 and 700, upright and italic) and then subset to Latin, Latin
Extended and common punctuation. Under the OFL these are Modified Versions,
distributed under the same licence and not using the Reserved Font Names of
